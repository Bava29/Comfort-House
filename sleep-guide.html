STEP 01 — KNOW YOUR SLEEP

Start With The Way
You Sleep.

Your sleeping position can help you understand the type of support and comfort that may work best for you.

01

Side Sleeper

Look for gentle pressure relief around your shoulders and hips with comfortable support.

02

Back Sleeper

A balanced feel can help with comfort support while keeping your body well supported.

03

Front Sleeper

A supportive, slightly firmer feel may provide the stable comfort you prefer while sleeping.

STEP 02 — FIND YOUR FEEL

How Firm Should
Your Mattress Feel?

Mattress firmness is all about how your body feels when you lie down. Understanding these three common feels can make choosing easier.

01

Soft

A softer feel that gently cushions your body and creates a relaxed, cosy sleeping experience.

Explore Soft Comfort
02

Balanced

A comfortable middle ground that combines cushioning with comfort for everyday sleep.

Explore Balanced Comfort
03

Firm

A more stable feel designed for deep sleepers who prefer stronger, grounded support.

Explore Firm Comfort
STEP 03 — KNOW YOUR MATERIAL

What Is Inside
Matters Too.

Different mattress materials create different feels. Understanding the basics can help you choose with confidence.

01

Memory Foam

Soft, contouring comfort that responds to your body and helps create a cushioned feel.

02

Latex

Naturally responsive comfort with a supportive feel and a little more bounce.

03

Spring

Traditional supportive comfort with a responsive feel and good airflow.

04

Hybrid

A combination of foam comfort and spring support for a balanced sleeping experience.
